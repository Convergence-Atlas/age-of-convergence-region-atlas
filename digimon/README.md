# Digimon: Echoes of the First Light

An unofficial fan campaign and illustrated reference, centered on Digimon Adventure and Adventure 02, with optional later-series and movie branches.

Open `index.html` for the complete companion. The included snapshot has 1,561 pictured Digimon forms, source-listed moves and evolution links, 82 locations with 328 area views, Digitama, Armor Digi-Eggs, crests, Digivices, quests, named cast, NPCs and villains.

Use `Digimon_Echoes_of_the_First_Light_World.json` for the full world export. The copy helper and section ZIP provide all 49 sections separately.

Weaknesses, resistances, tactical effects, costs, Ultra progression, and Miracles/Destiny campaign Tags are original fan-game rules. Human-led temporary evolution requires a compatible bonded Digivice. Normal reversion returns to the partner's earned Rookie base; severe depletion and established resting-form exceptions retain their stated rules.

Digimon characters, names and source artwork belong to their respective creators. Environment illustrations and campaign prose are original additions. Catalog entries link their sources. This is a retrieved roster snapshot, not a guarantee that every future or obscure promotional form is covered.
